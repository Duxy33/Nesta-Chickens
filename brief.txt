Nesta Chickens – Project Brief
Introduction / Executive Summary
Nesta Chickens is a pioneering poultry farming business located in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe.
The farm focuses on raising free■range, organic chickens and producing high■quality
eggs that are GMO■free and raised ethically. Our commitment to sustainable agriculture
allows us to deliver nutritious and flavorful poultry products to households, retailers, and
institutions across the region. This project brief outlines our brand, product lines, market
positioning, and communication strategy.
Background & Context
Established with the vision of providing healthier alternatives to industrially produced
poultry, Nesta Chickens has built its reputation on trust, transparency, and care for both
animals and the environment. We operate on the principle that healthy chickens raised in
a natural environment yield better■tasting and healthier meat and eggs. Our farm in
Cowdry Park, Bulawayo, is designed to accommodate day■old chicks, hatchery
operations, and grown bird rearing.
Mission: To supply ethically raised poultry and eggs that promote health, sustainability,
and superior taste.
Vision: To become the most trusted poultry brand in Zimbabwe and beyond.
Values: Animal welfare, sustainability, organic practices, and customer satisfaction.

Brand Identity & Colour Palette
Our brand identity represents vitality, quality, and natural growth. The rooster logo
symbolizes leadership, energy, and freshness.
Colour Palette:

- Vibrant Red (#D32F2F) – Passion, energy, and trust
- Orange/Yellow (#FFA000) – Warmth, nourishment, vitality

- Dark Grey (#4A4A4A) – Professionalism and subtle elegance
Typography is modern and clean, reflecting both tradition and innovation.
Product Lines & Portfolio
Day■Old Chicks: We provide robust and healthy chicks to farmers, supported with
vaccination and after■sales guidance.
Hatchery / Fertile Eggs: Our hatchery ensures high fertility rates, quality storage, and
professional distribution to small and large farmers.
Grown■Up Birds: Raised under humane and organic conditions, our birds are sold live or
processed, catering to households, restaurants, and retailers.
Nature Chickens: Premium heritage birds raised slower, in free■range systems for richer
flavor and superior nutrition.
Eggs & By■Products: Fresh organic eggs available in different packaging sizes, plus
manure by■products for organic fertilizers.

Target Market & Customer Segments
Our diverse customer base includes:
- Local households seeking organic meat and eggs
- Retail outlets and supermarkets requiring consistent supply
- Restaurants, hotels, and catering companies looking for premium poultry
- Farmers and hatcheries purchasing chicks and fertile eggs
- Health■conscious consumers who prefer natural, chemical■free foods
Marketing & Communication Strategy
Brand Messaging: We emphasize transparency, freshness, and sustainability with
messages like “Naturally Raised, Ethically Yours.”
Marketing Channels: Our outreach strategy includes a strong digital presence via our
website, Facebook, and Instagram pages, as well as WhatsApp for direct customer
engagement and orders. Offline strategies include flyers, community markets, and
partnerships with local retailers.
Customer Engagement: We maintain open communication through farm tours, customer
feedback sessions, and recipe sharing. WhatsApp has become a vital channel for orders,
updates, and customer care.

Sustainability & Community Engagement
Sustainability is a cornerstone of Nesta Chickens. We utilize poultry manure for organic
composting, minimizing waste and promoting eco■friendly farming. Our free■range
system reduces reliance on industrial feed and promotes healthier growth cycles.
Additionally, Nesta Chickens contributes to the local economy by creating employment
opportunities and supporting smallholder farmers with affordable chicks and eggs.
Future Goals & Expansion
Looking forward, Nesta Chickens aims to scale operations to meet growing demand,
invest in modern hatchery equipment, and expand distribution channels. Plans are in place
to explore export opportunities for fertile eggs and establish partnerships with regional
retailers. We are committed to becoming a household name across Zimbabwe and
expanding into regional markets like South Africa and Botswana.
